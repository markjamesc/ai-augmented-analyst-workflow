# Becoming an AI-Augmented Analyst

This portfolio artifact presents my five-stage analytical workflow for turning technical reporting into decision support:

1. **Start** - identify the business decision behind the metric request.
2. **Framing** - turn vague stakeholder language into one precise decision.
3. **Design** - define the hypothesis, KPI, analytical grain, segments, confounders, and measurement risks before writing SQL.
4. **Execution** - produce evidence with SQL, modular R/Tidyverse workflows, interpretable modeling, Excel outputs, and dashboards, using AI as a quality-control layer.
5. **Finish** - distinguish what the evidence supports from what it does not support and recommend a proportionate next action.

The objective is not merely faster reporting. It is better decisions grounded in transparent evidence. AI assists with structure, analytical design, review, and quality control; human judgment remains responsible for the evidence, interpretation, and recommendation.
