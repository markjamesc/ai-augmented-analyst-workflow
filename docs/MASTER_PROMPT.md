# Master prompt: lean five-stage analysis

Use the current linked stage documents and one authoritative design.json. Do not reconstruct older requirements from historical documents. GrokBot coordinates; ChatGPT drafts and builds R-A; DeepSeek builds R-B; Grok performs the single independent Design review and validation oversight. Finish uses ChatGPT synthesis, Grok inference review and DeepSeek numerical/evidence review. Each designated review occurs once; verify only affected content after a substantive correction.

Start/Framing identify the owner, decision and one answerable question. Combine context confirmation into substantive prompts. Synthetic stakeholder answers are labeled synthetic and cannot be described as independent real-world evidence.

Design contains the question, population/grain, definitions, filters, decision rule, source identity, frozen fixture oracle, output/missingness contracts, relevant safeguards and model declaration. One independent reviewer checks design meaning and feasible implementation. One revision resolves findings. The reviewer verifies substantive resolutions without repeating a full audit. Owner approves the exact contract hash. No file-per-field provenance or duplicate warrants.

Execution confirms SQL/source correctness and independently implemented R wrangling. The checker is versioned and pretested before live work. Never construct a new harness during the live run. Give builders only their specification and permitted inputs; never show the peer solution. Known answers stay coordinator/reviewer-side. Preserve actual prompts/code and a short input/exposure declaration in the run manifest. Identical parsing idioms do not fail a build. Confirmed prohibited copied logic or a real defect requires repair.

Finish uses only current validated evidence. Pair material claims with exact evidence and interpretation. Exploratory unvalidated modeling is excluded from the decision and headline. Evaluate applicable methodological safeguards; numerical agreement does not establish causal identification, predictive validity or profitable action.

## Repair and authority

Fix tools and rerun affected checks. Do not infer wrong analysis from a crash, missing script or incompatible runtime. A substantive wrong result requires remediation. Escalate after THREE unsuccessful repair attempts on the SAME defect; changing author/chat does not reset the count. The limit is operational, not analytical failure; no check is waived. Keep one exception/repair log and all failed attempt directories.

Routine invocations/path corrections and byte-identical restoration are delegated to GrokBot. Other code/configuration changes return to ChatGPT; changed harness dependencies/config/interfaces require affected pretests again. Changed analytical decisions require owner approval and a successor run/contract; retain predecessor evidence. Usage/access outages pause safely. PC offline: retry every five minutes up to three times, then report. Silence is never new authority.

Reserved owner touchpoints: Stage-3 lock, three failed repairs, changed locked decisions, and publishing. No main changes or PR/merge without separate approval. No unnecessary owner confirmations, deadlines, nonce or FILE-line gates. Wrong/stale/truncated inputs are still corrected before use.
